Housing shouldn’t be the hardest part of your degree.

Nimbus is a student accommodation marketplace built in Gaborone, for students in Gaborone.

Our mission: make finding a safe, fairly-priced home near campus as simple, honest, and stress-free as it should have been all along.

Why we built it

Every semester the same scramble repeats — WhatsApp groups full of half-real listings, deposits sent to strangers, wasted combi trips across town to see a room that doesn’t match the photos, and prices that climb the moment someone hears “student.” It costs students money they don’t have and time they can’t spare.

Nimbus replaces that with verified listings, transparent pricing, real commute information, and an escrow system so your deposit is never at the mercy of a stranger’s honesty.

How we’re different

Verified

Real homes, checked by our team — not anonymous posts.

Protected

Escrow holds your money until you confirm the room at the door.

Smart

Combi-route scoring by Heisenberg AI (Nubia × Nimbus) ranks homes by your real daily commute.

Built with Nubia

Nimbus shares its routing intelligence with Nubia, our student platform. The “Heisenberg AI” that scores how easy each home’s combi trip to campus really is is powered by Nubia’s engine — one brain, two products, both in service of students.

Where we’re going

We’re starting with Gaborone’s student neighbourhoods and the campuses around them, then expanding to moving help, prepaid utilities, and a portal universities can offer their own students. If that sounds like something you want to exist, join the waitlist.
