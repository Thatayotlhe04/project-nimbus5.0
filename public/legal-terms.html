Eligibility

You must be at least 18 (or have guardian consent) and able to enter a binding agreement. Nimbus is intended for students and landlords in Gaborone and surrounding areas.

What Nimbus is

Nimbus is a marketplace connecting students with landlords and an escrow facilitator for booking payments. We are not the landlord and do not own the listed properties.

Bookings & escrow

  • When you book, your first month’s rent and refundable deposit are held in escrow by Nimbus.
  • Funds are released to the landlord only after you confirm move-in at the property.
  • If the property is materially not as described, you may dispute and request a refund of escrowed funds, subject to our review.

Fees

  • Students: a one-time service fee of 8% of one month’s rent (minimum P150, maximum P300), charged when a booking is approved. It is non-refundable once the booking is confirmed.
  • Landlords: a 6% commission on rent collected through Nimbus, deducted from the payout.

Payments

Payments are made via Orange Money, MyZaka, or Smega. You are responsible for the accuracy of your payment details and for any provider charges.

Landlord obligations

Landlords warrant that listings are accurate, that they have the right to let the property, and that they will honour confirmed bookings. False or unsafe listings may be removed and accounts suspended.
