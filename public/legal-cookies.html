Cookie Policy

Last updated: 28 May 2026 · Gaborone, Botswana
⚠️ This is a starter template, not legal advice. Have a Botswana-qualified lawyer review it before you rely on it, and tailor it to how Nimbus actually operates.

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Nimbus uses a small number of them, explained below. You can accept or limit non-essential cookies via the banner.

Essential cookies

  • nimbus_sid — a session identifier that keeps your visit consistent and attributes a booking to you.
  • nimbus_variant — remembers which version of our landing page you saw, so it doesn’t change on you.
  • nimbus_cookie_consent — remembers your cookie choice.

These are required for the site to work and are set without consent on a strictly-necessary basis.

Analytics / experimentation

We measure whether people who land on the site go on to join the waitlist, to improve our messaging (A/B testing). This relies on the session and variant cookies above; we don’t use third-party advertising cookies.

Managing cookies

Choose “Essential only” in the banner to limit non-essential use, or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking essential cookies may break bookings.

Changes

We’ll update this page if our cookie use changes.
