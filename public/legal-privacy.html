Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 May 2026 · Gaborone, Botswana
⚠️ This is a starter template, not legal advice. Have a Botswana-qualified lawyer review it before you rely on it, and tailor it to how Nimbus actually operates.

This policy explains what personal information Nimbus collects, why, and your rights. It is intended to align with Botswana’s Data Protection Act, 2018.

Who we are

Nimbus is a student-accommodation marketplace operating in Gaborone, Botswana. For privacy questions, contact privacy@nimbus.example.

What we collect

  • Account & waitlist: your email address, and a name and phone number if you make a booking.
  • Bookings: the listing, move-in date, amounts, chosen payment method, and a payment reference.
  • Device & usage: a session identifier, your A/B test variant, IP address and basic analytics, stored in cookies (see our Cookie Policy).
  • Location of listings only: we use property and campus coordinates to estimate commutes. We do not track your live location.

How we use it

  • To provide search, commute estimates, bookings, and escrow.
  • To process payments via mobile-money providers and settle funds.
  • To improve the product (including A/B testing of the landing page).
  • To communicate with you about your waitlist place or a booking.

Who we share it with

  • Payment providers (Orange Money, MyZaka, Smega) and our bank, to collect and settle payments.
  • Infrastructure providers (e.g. our database host) that process data on our behalf under contract.
  • Nubia’s Heisenberg engine, to score combi convenience — we send only listing/campus identifiers and coordinates, never your personal details.

We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

We keep booking and payment records as long as needed for the service and to meet legal/accounting obligations, then delete or anonymise them. Waitlist emails are kept until you ask us to remove them.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may withdraw consent for non-essential cookies at any time. Email privacy@nimbus.example.

Security

We use access controls and encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you of a breach as required by law.

Changes

We’ll post any changes here and update the date above.
