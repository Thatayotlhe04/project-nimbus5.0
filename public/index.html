Not seeing your question? Drop us a line when you join the waitlist.

When does Nimbus launch?
We're rolling out around the start of the 2026 academic intake, beginning with UB and BAC. Waitlist members get access two weeks before the public launch — and lock in founder pricing.
Is it actually free for students?
Yes. Browsing, filtering, chatting, and booking are free for students. Landlords pay a small listing fee — and we don't take a cut of your rent.
How do you verify landlords?
Every landlord submits ID and proof of ownership or lease authority. Every property is visited in person by our team before going live. If anything doesn't check out, it doesn't get listed. Period.
What if I have an issue after I move in?
Open a ticket in-app and our support team mediates with the landlord. Deposits sit in escrow with Nimbus during the first month, so you have real leverage if something's off.
Can I list my place on Nimbus?
Yes — we're onboarding landlords now, especially in Block 6, 7, 8, and around BAC. Get in touch through the waitlist form and pick "I'm a landlord" in the follow-up email.
